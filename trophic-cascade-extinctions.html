Trophic Co-extinction Cascade Model

Reproduction of the food-chain model from Amaral & Meyer, “Environmental Changes, Co-extinction, and Patterns in the Fossil Record” (1998, arXiv:cond-mat/9804102). Species occupy niches across trophic levels; level 0 is autotrophic, higher levels each feed on up to k random prey in the level below. Each tick: a random fraction p of level-0 species go extinct from environmental change, extinction then cascades upward through broken food links; separately, existing species speciate at rate µ into empty neighboring niches. No competition or fitness is modelled — mass extinctions emerge purely from cascading co-extinction.
